Fixed the Play button sometimes doing nothing after the last song in the queue had finished, for example after the sleep timer. Pressing Play now restarts that song from the beginning.
Fixed playback from a restored session sometimes not responding, or jumping back to the earlier song after you had already picked another one.
Fixed album artwork sometimes not appearing in the full player. If the large artwork fails to load, the player now falls back to the regular artwork instead of leaving a blank space.
Fixed lyrics not being found for songs that were uploaded as videos, where the artist name appears inside the video title instead of the artist field. The lyrics lookup now also tries the artist and title written in the title itself.
Sleep timer: while a timer is running, the sheet now shows the time left as a large live countdown, and the timer icon is now the Bedtime moon from the design frames, with a filled moon on the active timer.
Sleep timer: skipping to the next or previous song no longer cancels a running timer. The countdown keeps running while you skip, and an end-of-song timer stays set for the song you land on. Picking a different song or playlist to play still cancels the timer, as before.
Crossfade: if a song's stream is refused during a crossfade, the app now switches that song to a backup playback path so the music keeps playing instead of going silent. Only the refused song loses its fade; the rest still crossfade as before.
Fixed a repeated update prompt: after updating, Clai no longer offers you the version you already have, and the Updates screen now correctly tells you when you're on the latest version.
Fixed the playback waveform and the player's breathing glow sometimes staying empty for a whole session. Clai now asks for the microphone permission it needs to measure the music as it plays (it never records or stores any audio) and starts measuring as soon as you allow it, with no restart needed. This also fixes volume normalization never learning how loud your songs are.
